Meet Your Hosts

Angela & Marta

We’re Angela and Marta, a mother and daughter sharing our love for El Salvador. Marta was born and raised in El Salvador, and Angela is American-Salvadoran. Together, we created Villa del Sol Garden as a welcoming home by the beach where guests can slow down, reconnect with nature, and experience the warmth of Salvadoran hospitality. Here we celebrate local art, culture, wellness and community. It’s our joy to share this special place with you.

Antonio, our trusted on-site caretaker, is here to ensure your stay is comfortable and carefree. Whether you need a recommendation or a helping hand, we’re all here to help you feel right at home.

House Agreements

  • A peaceful retreat. We cultivate a quiet, wellness-focused environment where guests can relax, recharge, and connect with nature. Please save celebrations and louder gatherings for the beach.
  • Help us care for our planet. Please turn off the air conditioning and lights whenever you leave your room to help conserve energy.
  • Keep our kitchen welcoming for everyone. After cooking, please wash your dishes, wipe down surfaces, and store all food to help keep the space fresh and free of insects.
  • A local plumbing note. To protect our plumbing system, please place toilet paper in the provided bin rather than flushing it. Thank you for helping us care for the property.
  • Enjoy music mindfully. Feel free to listen to music at a medium volume so everyone can enjoy the atmosphere.
  • Ask about a day pass to extend your stay to 5:00 pm.

Things to Do

Around the villa

A quick look at the walk down to the beach.

  • The beach is a 5-minute walk (depending on your pace), across the road and a few houses
  • Take a boat trip on the Estero mangroves (about 15 minutes away)
  • Watch the sunset over the Pacific
  • Visit the local market and pupuserias
  • Use the yoga and art studio and the pool
  • A pharmacy is nearby and a church is within walking distance
