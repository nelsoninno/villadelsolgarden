Good to Know

Frequently asked questions

How far is the airport?

About 30 minutes by car from San Salvador International Airport (SAL). Rental cars and pickup/dropoff transfers available for an additional fee.

How far is the beach?

The beach is just a 5–10 minute walk away, depending on your pace. The Estero mangroves are about a 15-minute walk to the water's edge, or a quick 10-minute drive to the nearest marina.

Can I book the whole villa for a group?

Yes. The Garden Collection is all six rooms, sleeping up to 16 guests, from $425 for four rooms on the first level to $650 for all six rooms on both levels.

Is it good for retreats?

Yes, it is built for them: a yoga and art studio, a sound sala, a pool, a chef, and local support. We co-host so you can lead.

What is included, and what costs extra?

Coffee is included with every stay, and breakfast is included for reservations of 2 nights or more. Lunch, massages, boat trips, fishing, and airport pickup/dropoff are optional add-ons.

Are there restaurants nearby?

A local market and pupuserias are minutes away walking, plus beach restaurants like Nazca at Tortuga Village, La Ola Beto, Tesoro Beach and Estero restaurants like Nammu at Astra Beach Club, Mar y Sol, La Pampa, and more.
