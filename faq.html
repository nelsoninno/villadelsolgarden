Frequently asked questions

The area and getting here

How far is Villa del Sol Garden from the airport?

About 30 minutes by car from San Salvador International Airport (SAL), on the Costa del Sol near La Herradura.

How far is the beach?

The Pacific is about a 5-minute walk, across the road and a few houses. The Estero mangroves are around 15 minutes on foot.

Are there restaurants nearby?

A local market and pupuserias are minutes away, plus beach restaurants like Astra, Tortuga Village, La Ola Beto, and Tesoro Beach.

Is it a good place for solo women travelers?

Yes. Many solo women have stayed and felt safe and cared for, with a host and caretaker on site.

How do we get there from the airport?

A 30-minute drive along the Costa del Sol. We can arrange an airport pickup on request.

The rooms and the villa

What rooms are available?

Six garden suites across two levels, four downstairs and two larger ones upstairs, named La Semilla, Piedra Luna, Tierra Roja, and Montana de Cafe.

Can a third person fit into a room?

The larger upstairs suites can add a bed for up to four guests. Downstairs suites are best for two.

Can we reserve the whole villa for a big group?

Yes. The Garden Collection is all six suites, sleeping up to 16, from 425 US dollars per night for the first floor or 550 for both floors.

Is the pool or kitchen shared?

The garden pool and kitchen serve house guests. Book a single suite and you may share with other guests; book the Collection and the whole garden is yours.

What amenities are there?

A private pool, a yoga and art studio, an outdoor kitchen, hot water, air conditioning, Wi-Fi, local art, and musical instruments on site.

Stays, retreats and prices

How much does it cost?

Rooms start from 100 US dollars per night and the whole garden from 425. Book direct with a best-price guarantee.

What is included, and what costs extra?

Breakfast is included with every stay. Lunch, massages, boat trips, fishing, and airport pickup are optional add-ons.

Can I host a retreat?

Yes. The villa is a retreat venue with a yoga and art studio, a sound sala, a chef, and a co-host. You lead; we host the ground.

Who is it for, and who is it not for?

It is for relaxation seekers, nature and bird lovers, solo travelers, couples, families, artists, and retreat organizers. It is not for party-goers.

Do you offer longer or monthly stays?

Yes, a monthly rate is available for longer stays. Just ask.

House and good to know

What are the house rules?

A wellness space: no heavy drinking or parties, music kept low, energy conserved, and please do not flush toilet paper (a bin is provided).

Is there Wi-Fi?

Yes, Wi-Fi throughout the villa.

What are the sounds like?

Birdsong and roosters, gentle local life, and a school across the street. Peaceful, not silent.

What can I buy on site?

Local cacao, coffee, and artisanal mugs are for sale.
