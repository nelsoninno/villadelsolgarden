# villadelsolsv.com - your website package

Last updated July 2026.

## What's here
This folder is your whole website. `index.html` is the English home page and
`es/index.html` is the Spanish one. Your photos and logo live under
`assets/images/`, the hero video under `assets/video/`, and your colors and fonts
live in one file, `assets/css/tokens.css`.

## Replace the photos
Drop your originals into `_source/photos-original/` (any format, I will convert
them). The web-ready versions go under `assets/images/` in the right folder:

| Drop it as | Where it appears | Goes to | Recommended size |
|---|---|---|---|
| your best villa shot | Top of the page (hero) | `assets/images/hero/` | min 1920 wide |
| villa / garden photos | The Villa gallery | `assets/images/gallery/` | min 1400 wide |
| room / group / retreat shots | Ways to Stay cards | `assets/images/rooms/` | min 1200 wide |
| your logo | Header / footer | `assets/images/logos/` | square, vector if possible |

## Change your colors or fonts
All colors and fonts are in `assets/css/tokens.css`. Editing that one file
re-skins the whole site.

## The hero video
The looping video at the top is a light, muted, web-optimized version
(about 1.3 MB) built from your main video, with the bathroom clip and the audio
removed. The full original stays in `_source/video-original/`.

## Request a change
Message me with what you would like changed. Small text and photo changes are
quick. The site is bilingual, so every change is made in both English and Spanish.
