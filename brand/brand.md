# Villa del Sol Garden - Brand Sheet

The plain-language companion to `assets/css/tokens.css`. Keep the two in agreement.

## Voice
Warm, calm, boutique. Peace, rest, and transformation. A wellness space by the sea,
never loud or "party." Speaks to relaxation seekers, solo women travelers, couples,
families, artists, and retreat organizers. Bilingual (English + Spanish), grounded in
El Salvador and the Costa del Sol.

## Colors (hex live in tokens.css)
- Cream ground `--bg` #F8F3E8 and deeper cream `--surface` #F1E8D6 (page + alternate sections)
- Deep forest green `--forest` #234A39 (headings, brand) and `--forest-deep` #1B3A2D (footer)
- Warm gold `--gold` #C39A3D (buttons, links) and `--gold-deep` #A97F2C (hover)
- Coral botanical accent `--coral` #D98A6B (used sparingly)
- Warm near-black `--ink` #2B2A24 (body text), muted `--muted` #6E695C

Palette is taken directly from the logo: cream, forest green, gold, with coral and
leaf-yellow botanical accents.

## Fonts (Google Fonts)
- Display / headings: Fraunces (a warm serif) - weights 400, 500, 600
- Body: Nunito Sans - weights 400, 600, 700, 800

## Logo
`assets/images/logos/logo-villa-del-sol-garden-villadelsolsv.com.webp` - the sun,
botanical branch, and wave emblem with the "Villa del Sol / Garden / At the Beach"
wordmark on cream. Square crop used for favicons.

## Do / Don't
- Do: keep generous whitespace, subtle motion (the hero video, gentle scroll reveals),
  real photography of the villa, gardens, ocean, and hosts.
- Don't: use em-dashes anywhere visible, imply guests can book the owner's private house
  (only the six-suite guest garden is rentable), or use a loud, "party" tone.
