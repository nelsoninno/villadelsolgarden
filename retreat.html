The Garden Retreat

You lead, we host

Host your retreat at Villa del Sol Garden. You bring your program and community, and we take care of the logistics, culinary options, and local support. With varying chef options and customizable add-ons such as sound baths, massages, boat tours, and more, you can create a seamless experience while staying fully present with your guests.

  • A yoga and art studio
  • A sound sala for sound healing, circles, and ceremony
  • The whole garden, pool, and kitchen for your group
  • A chef and meal plans
  • Airport pickups and local transport
  • We co-host the ground so you can lead the room
